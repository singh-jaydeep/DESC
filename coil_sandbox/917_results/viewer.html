Length, curvature and clearance are the dense-check values against the Kruger et al. bounds: L ≤ 36 m per coil, κ ≤ 1.5 /m, d_cc and d_pc ≥ 1.2 m — except Polar B=2, L ≤ 37 m, which was optimised against a deliberately relaxed length cap and so is not comparable on L, and FourierXYZ, 16 coils, which has 4 unique coils instead of 3 and therefore 576 m of conductor against 432 m — it is not a controlled test of coil count alone.
