## 1. Boundary repair (`../sandbox/bdry_repair/`)

**Symptom.** B·n and QS are blind to how the boundary is parametrized, so during free-boundary or single-stage solves
the poloidal angle can stall: the area element goes to zero at a spot, curvature reaches 1e4-4e5 /m, the edge Jacobian
goes negative, and B·n shows a 10-20% spike at one point.

**What was tried.** DESC PR 2337 (`rc/surface_fit`) fits Fourier coefficients and per-point angle labels jointly.
Used unguarded, it returns to the creased angle, because that is the only parametrization that reproduces the old
surface exactly. What works: equal-arclength labels, then 1-3 label steps, each kept only if the area element and
curvature stay healthy and the fit error still drops.

**Pipeline.** Gate (skip healthy boundaries) → relabelled points → guarded fit → interior re-solved by continuation →
verify, with fallback to fewer label steps. Use: `from bdry_repair import repair; eq, info = repair(eq)`.

Surface displacement of the refit, mm (rms / 99th percentile / max), basis M = N = 12:

| boundary | old repair | new repair |
|---|---|---|
| ι 0.42 start, creased | 0.67 / 3.3 / 5.2 | 0.12 / 0.56 / 1.5 |
| clean boundary after k = 1 | 0.39 / 1.6 / 3.8 | 0.016 / 0.05 / 0.10 (gate skips it) |
| design B, creased | 0.22 / 1.0 / 1.7 | 0.09 / 0.36 / 1.2 |

Effect of the full repair (QS error at ρ = 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1, %):

| case | QS before | QS after (new) | QS after (old) | max B·n before → after | edge Jacobian before → after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ι 0.42 start | 0.35 / 0.67 / 1.31 / 2.37 | 0.29 / 0.71 / 1.34 / 2.37 | 0.90 / 1.09 / 1.78 / 3.62 | 7.3% → 0.88% | −0.10 → +0.57 |
| design B | 0.25 / 0.28 / 0.47 / 0.89 | 0.25 / 0.28 / 0.47 / 0.95 | not run | 22.6% → 0.44% | −0.10 → +0.65 |

Limits: the repair does not stop a crease forming inside a single boundary shell; the continuation solve at basis 12
needs ~12 GB of GPU memory (marginal on the 16 GB laptop).

## 2. Design C: the ss4 redux (`ss/ref042r/`)

**Question.** Design A (ss4, ι ≈ 0.35) was made before the repair existed; design B (ι 0.22) was much better. Was B
better because of lower ι or because its solve was cleaner? Rerun A's parent (ι 0.42 near-axis state) with the repair.

**Run.** Modal job `ss12_ref042_q15r`: start repair, single stage k = 1..6 (QS tolerance 0.15 × start, ι ≥ 0.30,
aspect band 6.5-7.0), free-boundary rescore with shells 4 and 8. The gate found no repair needed after any k step.

| stage | QS at ρ .25 / .5 / .75 / 1 (%) | ι | B·n rms |
|---|---|---|---|
| single stage, k = 6 | 0.39 / 0.42 / 0.69 / 1.42 | 0.299-0.352 | 0.13% |
| free-boundary rescore | 0.36 / 0.45 / 0.74 / 1.51 | 0.296-0.354 | 0.19% |
| rescore, repaired locally | 0.37 / 0.48 / 0.74 / 1.49 | 0.293-0.349, non-monotonic | 0.23% (max 1.5%) |

**Answer.** C lands near A (0.42 / 0.43 / 0.84 / 1.74%), not B (0.25 / 0.28 / 0.47 / 0.94%). Most of B's gain is lower ι.

**Not resolved.** Both rescore shells ended on "A bad approximation caused failure to predict improvement" with B·n
stuck at 0.19%, and the boundary creased again in shell 8 (10.7% spot). The same question hangs over B's 0.10-0.14%.

## 4. Hinge scan: thin band and small tilt (`na/loose.sh`, runs `lo_qa2_i*_h*_t*`)

**Setup.** Near-axis, 4 coils per half period, bounds and second-order weights of the z = 0 best state. Both hinges of
every coil within |z| ≤ H (H = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 a; a = 0.167 in near-axis units); hinge line level, tilted ≤ 2°
(new `--tiltmax`), or free inside the band; ι target 0.30, 0.42, 0.50, each continued from its own z = 0 state.
All 27 runs converged.

Field cost relative to z = 0 at the same ι target (z = 0 costs: 0.0472, 0.0738, 0.0938):

| band | tilt | ι 0.30 | ι 0.42 | ι 0.50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 a | level | 0.87 | 0.85 | 0.86 |
| 0.1 a | ≤ 2° (= free; the band only permits ~1.8°) | 0.79 | 0.81 | 0.82 |
| 0.2 a | level | 0.68 | 0.71 | 0.71 |
| 0.2 a | ≤ 2° | 0.63 | 0.67 | 0.45 |
| 0.2 a | free (3.6-4.0° used) | 0.59 | 0.57 | 0.43 |
| 0.3 a | level | 0.61 | 0.55 | 0.50 |
| 0.3 a | ≤ 2° | 0.51 | 0.46 | 0.39 |
| 0.3 a | free (5.3-5.5° used) | 0.46 | 0.41 | 0.36 |

* Roughly linear in band height, no threshold. A 2° tilt captures most of the tilt gain.
* Higher ι benefits most: ι target 0.50 with 0.2 a and 2° reaches ι 0.48 at cost 0.042, below the z = 0 state at
  ι 0.30 (0.047, achieved ι 0.28).
* Achieved ι is under target in every run and rises as the band widens (0.372 → 0.403 at ι 0.42), so part of each
  cost reduction is ι catching up.
* Second-order residual B20 unchanged (0.35-0.52); the second-order gradient mismatch roughly halves.
* Correction to the earlier level-hinge scan (`NAE_HANDOFF.md` section 1): its heights 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20 are
  0.12 / 0.30 / 0.6 / 1.2 a, not the fractions quoted at the time.

## 5. Engineering-bound scan (`na/loose2.sh`, runs `l2_qa2_*`)

**Setup.** ι target 0.42, one knob at a time, at two hinge settings: z = 0 (base cost 0.0738) and band 0.2 a with
tilt ≤ 2° (base cost 0.0493). All 18 continuation runs converged.

* Length dominates and compounds with the band: both together give 0.013, 18% of the z = 0 base.
* Longer coils sit further from the plasma (minimum distance 0.885 a → 0.9-1.0 a).
* Curvature and coil-coil spacing matter mainly once the hinges are loose.
* Clearance buys almost nothing; with loose hinges the coils stop at 0.78 a and go no closer.
* B20: 0.40 → 0.28 for length ×1.15 at z = 0, but 0.35 at ×1.30 and no change with loose hinges. Not a consistent effect.

## What is and is not established

* **Established:** the repair numbers (section 1); designs A, B, C with free-boundary QS, ε_eff and Poincaré
  (sections 2-3).
* **Free-boundary scored but unconverged, no single stage (section 7):** the 6-coil z = 0 and 5-coil thin-band states.
* **Near-axis only (the rest of sections 4-6):** the cost is a first- plus second-order on-axis field mismatch with an ι miss
  folded in. By the project's scoring rule these are candidates until scored by a vacuum free-boundary solve with the
  coils. The second-order residual did not move in any scan, so the cost ratios may overstate the gain in QS error.
* **Open:** the free-boundary rescore stall at B·n ≈ 0.19%; no height cap or tilt bound in `ss/single_stage.py`;
  coil count combined with longer coils not tried; nfp 3 not tried with any relaxation.

* Both relaxed states roughly halve the QS error of the 4-coil near-axis state at every radius, and cut it fourfold
  near the axis (0.08-0.09% at ρ = 0.25). Before any single stage they already beat designs A and C at higher ι
  (0.37-0.40), and beat B in the core.
* The two relaxations score about the same: 6 coils on z = 0 ≈ 5 coils with the thin band.
* The edge (ρ = 1) is still at 1.2-1.3%, as the unchanged second-order residual suggested.
* B·n fell every shell (6 coils: 0.58 → 0.36 → 0.31 → 0.19%; 5 coils: 0.69 → 0.49 → 0.27 → 0.16%). Shells 4 and 6
  hit the iteration limit and shell 8 ended on "A bad approximation caused failure to predict improvement", so
  neither solve is converged. The boundary creased in shells 4 and 6 of both runs (repaired, 0.08-0.24 mm rms) and
  the final shell-8 boundary is **not** repaired: the 4.4% and 10.5% B·n maxima are probably crease spots.
* Poincaré: 6 coils, the boundary follows the traced surfaces closely with nested surfaces beyond it. 5 coils, the
  boundary visibly cuts across traced surfaces in several sections, so its QS numbers are less trustworthy.
* **Coil-coil distance is violated** on the exact DESC coils: 0.0342 (6 coils) and 0.0290 (5 coils) against 0.04,
  where the near-axis code reported 0.0400. The 4-coil reference converts at 0.0400, so the near-axis soft-min is
  missing close approaches that appear with more coils or tilted hinges. Curvature is 1.4% over. A single stage
  with the branch's distance rows would have to fix this, at some cost in field quality.
