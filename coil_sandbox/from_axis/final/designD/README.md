# Design D: a quasi-axisymmetric stellarator with demountable two-face coils (5 per half period)

A vacuum QA stellarator (NFP 2, aspect ratio 7.56, iota 0.40 -> 0.35) and its coils. Each of the 20 coils is two
planar faces joined at two hinges, and every hinge lies within +-0.0334 m (0.27 a) of the midplane, with the hinge
line tilted at most 2 degrees. So the coil set comes apart along a thin horizontal band.

The coils are primary. The equilibrium is the vacuum **free-boundary** solution of these coils, and field-line tracing
of the coils confirms it independently.

| rotational transform | 0.399 (axis) -> 0.350 (edge), monotonic; traced and equilibrium iota agree to 7e-4 |
| quasisymmetry, L2 of the breaking Boozer modes | 0.24 / 0.21 / 0.35 / 0.78 % at rho .25 / .5 / .75 / 1 |
| largest breaking mode | 0.18 / 0.09 / 0.19 / 0.32 % |
| eps_eff | 2.6e-4 / 2.2e-4 / 7.2e-4 at rho .25 / .5 / .75 |
| Gamma_c (Nemov / Velasco), rho 0.5 | 7.6e-3 / 3.5e-3 |
| coils' B.n on the boundary | 1.6e-5 rms, 9.4e-5 max (of <\|B\|>) |
| coils | 5 unique, 20 total; **feasible**: length 0.9998, curvature 0.998, coil-coil 1.03, coil-plasma 1.15 of their bounds |
| islands | a 4/11 chain at rho ~0.815, ~1.7 mm wide; nothing else resolved; 2/5 is not in the plasma |

Bounds: the reactor set of Gil et al. (PRE 114, 025202, Table III), scaled by the near-axis minor radius 0.167 m:
coil length <= 3.941 m, curvature <= 10.17 /m, coil-coil >= 0.04 m, coil-plasma >= 0.148 m (`data/bounds.json`).

## Caveats

* **Vacuum only.** Finite beta, coil forces and a finite winding pack are not yet assessed (handoff step 3).
* The free-boundary solve stopped at its iteration limit while still improving (B.n 1.6e-5). Re-solving the interior
  at basis 16 moves QS and eps_eff by 1-4%.
* The coils come from a single-stage optimization whose equilibrium was resolved at basis 12. That resolution
  misreports iota by up to 0.05, so the single stage's iota >= 0.35 constraint was only approximate. The final state
  was re-scored at basis 14, and those are the numbers above.
* Gamma_c is converged only to about +-20%. eps_eff at the two resolutions agrees to within 9%.
* The coils reach out to R ~ 2 m, about 2 R0, and the hinge lines span the whole cross-section of the device. The
  coil length bound (23.6 a) permits this.
