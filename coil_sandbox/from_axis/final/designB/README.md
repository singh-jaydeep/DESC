# Design B: a quasi-axisymmetric stellarator whose coils all split on one plane (4 per half period)

A vacuum QA stellarator (NFP 2, aspect ratio 7.26, iota 0.24 -> 0.22) and its coils. Each of the 16 coils is two
planar faces joined at two hinges, and **every hinge lies exactly on z = 0**. So the whole coil set separates into
an upper and a lower half along one joint plane, the only single plane that stellarator symmetry allows.

The coils are primary. The equilibrium is the vacuum **free-boundary** solution of these coils, and field-line tracing
of the coils confirms it independently.

Bounds: the reactor set of Gil et al. (PRE 114, 025202, Table III), scaled by the near-axis minor radius 0.167 m:
coil length <= 3.941 m, curvature <= 10.17 /m, coil-coil >= 0.04 m, coil-plasma >= 0.148 m (`data/bounds.json`).

## B and D

D (`../designD`) has better quasisymmetry at mid radius and beyond: L2 25% lower at rho 0.5-0.75, and eps_eff and
Gamma_c 1.4-3x lower. It also has 1.7x the rotational transform. B has the cleaner coil class: one joint plane
instead of a +-0.03 m band, and 4 coils per half period instead of 5. Side by side with the literature:
`../compare/COMPARE.md`.

## Caveats

* **Vacuum only.** Finite beta, coil forces and a finite winding pack are not yet assessed (handoff step 3).
* After the single stage, the coils were polished to feasibility with the equilibrium held fixed: the length was
  1.1 mm over its bound and is now 0.9999 of it. They were then re-scored in free boundary. The polish cost <= 2% in
  QS.
* The final free-boundary shell (20 iterations) stopped at its iteration limit while still improving (B.n 8.7e-6).
  Basis 14 vs 16 was checked on D (1-4%), not on this exact state.
* B's chain has gaps in its record: early steps ran from uncommitted code, and the root near-axis run predates the
  first commit. See `REPRODUCE.md`, "Known gaps".
* Gamma_c is converged only to about +-20%.
